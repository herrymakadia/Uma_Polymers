Benefits of Polyester Strap / PET Strap

The Polyester Strap that we manufacture and supply is globally recognized for handling Heavy Loads, Sharp Edges, High Break Strength, Low Elongation Capabilities, and UV resistance.

Long Tension Retention

It can hold high tension for a significantly longer time without slackening during transit.

High Operative Temperatures

Our Polyester Strap keeps its structural quality and integrity even at high temperatures.

High Shock Absorbency

Very high shock absorbent capacity and recovery, cushioning cargo against sudden road bumps.

Rust & Corrosion Free

Our Polyester Strap will never rust, corrode, or stain your valuable finished products.

No Rebound Hazard

Does not dangerous rebound while cutting under stress, ensuring complete operator safety.

Superior to Steel Straps

Substantially lighter than steel straps with lower failure rates and zero sharp cut hazards.

Versatile Application

Can be applied with or without buckles, and is easy to apply even in tight, confined spaces.

Low Maintenance Cost

Low cost of maintenance, independent machine operation, and available in multiple color options.
